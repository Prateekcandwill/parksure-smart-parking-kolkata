# Brag Plan: ParkSure

## What is this app?

ParkSure is a mobile-first smart-parking demo for Kolkata: find a live bay, see a demand-adjusted hourly rate, book and pay, then switch into the gate, lot or admin workspace — one account, four roles, one premium dark interface, shipping as a PWA and an Android app.

## The angle

Most parking apps stop at the booking screen. ParkSure keeps going: the attendant gets an ANPR gate console, the operator gets a live lot map and cash reconciliation, the admin gets pricing rules and a hash-chained audit trail. The video plays the tagline completely straight, then reveals that the "demo" is a full parking company — and ends on the project's own deadpan disclaimer, **"Demo mode: simulated data."**

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Big, quiet, confident: **"Park without the wait."** over the real place card for Park Street Centre — 5 of 12 bays lit gold, ₹60/hr, a "Simulated live" pulse. The promise is made before the viewer has time to argue.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **The Find screen, recreated at video scale** — real Kolkata lots (Park Street Centre, Salt Lake Sector V, Howrah Station Forecourt), real rates (₹60, ₹40, ₹35), live bay counts and demand badges arriving one by one.
- **The pricing breakdown** — ₹60 base becomes ₹80/hr through a 1.35× multiplier: "82% occupied · weekday evening peak". The multiplier counts up, the price re-lands, the Peak demand badge appears. This is the most specific, least generic thing the product does.
- **The role reveal** — four workspace cards land in sequence with their real descriptions: "Find, book and pay for a bay." / "Run the gate with one clear decision at a time." / "Keep the lot moving and resolve exceptions." / "Set the rules that run every place." The twist lands here.

## Outro / punchline

The ParkSure mark and wordmark. "Park without the wait." Then, small and dry: **"Demo mode: simulated data."** — the project's own line, which makes the whole four-workspace flex funnier rather than dishonest.

## User flow worth showing

Find a live bay → see the demand rate → book and pay → switch roles and run the gate, lot or admin side. The video shows the first three beats in the driver UI and the fourth as the role reveal.

- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the top-right gold glow breathes with low-frequency energy (±8% opacity), never a waveform.

- Audio-coupled moments: bay card lands (soft drop), result rows arrive (card placements), multiplier count-up (light glass tick), role cards (card placements), logo (soft bell).

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook / one place — 3.7s
Left: eyebrow "KOLKATA · SMART PARKING DEMO", headline "Park without the wait." Right: the Park Street Centre card — 12 bay squares with 5 lit gold, "5 of 12 free", ₹60 / hr, "Simulated live" dot, "Covered · CCTV · 24×7". Headline lands first, card follows.
Sequential/interaction: yes — headline words blur in, then the card rises and the five free bays light one after another.
Audio intent: confident, quiet arrival; the music carries it.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop when the card lands; tiny ticks on the five bay squares.
Music: warm bed enters.
Transition mood: clean zoom-through on the card → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Find / the list — 4.7s
Recreated Find screen at video scale: "Find parking" title, search field with "Park Street", chips (All / Free now / Covered / Camera), then three result rows with real names, distances, bay counts, prices and free-bay badges. Caption line under the panel: "Find a live bay. See the rate."
Sequential/interaction: yes — three rows arrive one by one, each with a card-placement sound; the "Free now" chip selects.
Audio intent: the product doing its job, no hype.
Audio-coupled idea: rows at beat points 4.23, 4.75, 5.28; chip click at 4.05.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: hard cut with a small scale settle → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Dynamic pricing — 4.2s
Full-frame pricing panel: "DYNAMIC PRICING" label; big ₹80 / hr with ₹60 struck through; badge "Peak demand · 1.35×"; reason "82% occupied · weekday evening peak"; an 82% occupancy bar; three mono rows (Base ₹60.00, Occupancy ×1.20, Peak +0.15) that resolve into the 1.35× multiplier. Caption: "The rate moves with demand."
Sequential/interaction: yes — multiplier counts 1.00 → 1.35, the price re-lands from ₹60 to ₹80, the badge pops.
Audio intent: precise, satisfying, like a good receipt.
Audio-coupled idea: light glass tick when the multiplier settles; no big hit.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — One account, four workspaces — 4.2s
Headline "One account. Four workspaces." Four cards land left to right: Driver, Attendant, Operator, Administrator, each with its real one-line description. The fourth card carries the violet operator accent.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four cards, one per beat, all holding on screen together at the end.
Audio intent: the reveal should feel earned, not loud.
Audio-coupled idea: four card placements at 13.18, 13.70, 14.22, 14.76.
Music: bed peaks here.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Outro — 2.7s
Centered ParkSure mark + wordmark. "Park without the wait." Small mono line "Kolkata · Web + Android". Then the dry kicker: "Demo mode: simulated data."
Sequential/interaction: yes — logo lands, tagline fades up, disclaimer arrives last.
Audio intent: settle; let the bell ring over the fading bed.
Audio-coupled idea: soft bell on the logo at 17.91, music fades out by 19.4.
Music: fade out.
Transition mood: cut to black at the end.
