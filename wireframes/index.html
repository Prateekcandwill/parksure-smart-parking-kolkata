04 — Layout notes

Grid & spacing

  • 390 × 844 frame, 22px side gutters, 8px base spacing.
  • Cards use a 12px radius; controls 8–10px; chips and badges pill.
  • One primary action per screen, always full-width or right-weighted.
  • Content scrolls under a fixed bottom tab bar.

Type hierarchy

  • Screen title 15–16px bold; card title 11px bold.
  • Body 9px, meta 8px, labels 7px uppercase tracked.
  • Price is the largest number on the detail screen.
  • Grayscale only — hierarchy comes from weight and scale.

States to design next

  • Empty search, no results, and offline map fallback.
  • Loading skeletons for cards and bay grid.
  • Error, refund pending and receipt-ready states.
  • Long names and 200% text zoom without overflow.

Accessibility

  • 48px minimum touch targets; 72px for primary CTAs.
  • Visible focus rings and labels that never rely on colour.
  • Bay availability has a text legend, not just fills.
  • Bottom tabs pair icon with a text label.
